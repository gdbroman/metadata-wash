Metadata Wash adapts lists of AI-generation metadata keys, AI generator tokens,
IPTC 2025.1 AI-disclosure fields, China TC260 AIGC markers, vendor trailer
markers and C2PA cloud-manifest repositories from this project
(src/remove_ai_watermarks/metadata.py and _internal/constants.py), translated
to Swift in AISignatures.swift and JPEGContainer.swift. No code from its
pixel-level or machine-learning watermark processing is included.
